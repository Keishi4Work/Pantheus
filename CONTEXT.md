# Pantheus — Landing Page Context

## What this is
A brand-first landing page for **Pantheus**. Goal of v1: a strong, high-end **first impression** — not lead conversion, not a full product tour. A foundation we build on later.

## Brand in one line
The system your factory actually fits in. Custom ERP for Vietnamese manufacturing — built around how you work, running on your own ground, answering your questions.

## What Pantheus is
A consulting-led ERP studio for manufacturing SMEs in Vietnam. We don't sell a generic product — we shape a system around each factory's real process, deploy it on-premise, and layer AI on top so managers can ask plain questions and get real answers.

## Audience & intent
- **Audience:** general / brand. Not targeted at one persona — projecting presence and credibility.
- **Languages:** bilingual VN + EN. Both must read native (see Content.md). Adapt lines where a literal translation feels weak.
- **Goal:** initial impression; expandable into deeper pages later.

## Positioning wedge
Collapse the false choice between *cheap generic ERP that doesn't fit* and *expensive custom ERP that breaks the budget*. Pantheus is **bespoke fit at a price that still makes sense** — but the page leads on **value and craft**, never on price.

## The five pillars (full copy in Content.md)
1. **Built around you** — your process is the spec; bespoke, not templated; you own it.
2. **Runs on your ground** — on-premise, offline-capable, full control, no cloud dependency.
3. **Outcomes, not modules** — every feature tied to a measurable number; we solve bottlenecks, not ship feature lists.
4. **Built for the floor** — usable by non-technical workers; adoption is where ERP dies, so we design for it.
5. **Ask your factory** — AI turns operations data into plain-language answers.

## Competitive frame
Against stock ERP (Odoo / SAP-tier): our edge is **fit, sovereignty, no per-seat lock-in, physical presence, and Vietnamese depth** — framed as *premium value*, not *cheaper*. Price stays a quiet supporting fact, never the hook.

## Tone & principles
- **Value over features** — sell the outcome, not the module.
- **High-end, quiet confidence** — restrained, engineered, classy. No hype clichés, no exclamation marks.
- **Price present but never the lead** — ownership framed as control, not as a bargain.
- **Bilingual native** — neither language reads like a translation of the other.
- **Proof withheld in v1** — no client names, logos, or case studies yet. Reserve a slot for them later.

## Constraints
- On-premise product reality (no "cloud-native SaaS" language).
- v1 = impression layer; copy and structure must extend cleanly into future pages.

## Out of scope (v1)
- Client names / case studies / testimonials (kept secret for now).
- Pricing tables or numbers.
- Deep product/feature specs.
